About

I am an undergraduate student double majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics at Chungnam National University, and an undergraduate researcher at CNU MADE Lab under Prof. Jongryul Lee.

I am currently researching the memory problem in online video vision-language models (VLMs). I enjoy exploring and studying topics in depth, and I am interested in geometric algorithms, diffusion models, and natural language processing.
